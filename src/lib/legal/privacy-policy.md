## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how `[Operator Name]` ("Collectium," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use Collectium (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Data Controller

The data controller for the Service is `[Operator Name]`, reachable at `[Contact Email]`. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, this Policy is intended to comply with the GDPR and UK GDPR.

## 3. Information We Collect

**a) Account information.** When you register, we collect your username and password (stored as a hash). If you provide an email address or display name, we collect that too.

**b) Profile and collection content.** Binders, display cases, item descriptions, layouts, and any other content you create. This includes images you upload (after optional client-side compression and background removal).

**c) Technical and usage data.** Basic technical information needed to operate the Service: IP address, browser type, device type, and request logs. We do not use third-party analytics.

**d) Cookies and similar technologies.** Only essential cookies required for authentication and session management. See Section 8.

We do **not** collect payment information (the Service has no paid features) and we do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- create and manage your account;
- store, render, and display your binders, display cases, and items (including to viewers of your public profile);
- process images you upload (storage only — processing happens in your browser);
- generate and resolve sharing links and QR codes;
- secure the Service, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms;
- respond to your requests and support inquiries;
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your content for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell your data.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Contract** — processing necessary to provide the Service you requested (account, storage, display).
- **Legitimate interests** — securing the Service, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining logs.
- **Consent** — where you choose to make content public or share it; you may withdraw consent by removing content or deleting your account.
- **Legal obligation** — where required by law.

## 6. Image Processing

Image compression and background removal occur **entirely in your browser** (client-side). Collectium does not send your images to any third-party background-removal service. Only the final images you choose to save are transmitted to and stored on our servers as part of your account.

You are responsible for reviewing processed images. The interactive manual-outlining fallback lets you define the area to keep; everything outside is erased.

## 7. Sharing and Public Visibility

- Profiles and collections are **public by default**. Anyone can view them via direct link or QR code.
- When you share an item, we generate a link (and QR code) that resolves to that item. Anyone with the link can view it.
- Because content is public, **third parties may view, download, or redistribute your images**. Collectium cannot control this. Do not upload content you do not want publicly visible.
- We do not publish your account credentials or private account settings.

## 8. Cookies

We use only **essential cookies** needed for authentication and session management (for example, a session cookie). We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. You can block cookies in your browser, but doing so may prevent you from logging in or using the Service.

## 9. Third-Party Processors

The Service is built on a backend-as-a-service provider (`[BaaS Provider]`) that may handle hosting, database, and file storage. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security practices. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data to law enforcement or regulators when required by law.

## 10. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** we retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- **Account deletion:** deletion is soft — content is hidden immediately and permanently purged after approximately **30 days**, during which you may cancel. You may request a data export before or during deletion.
- **Logs and backups:** technical logs and backups may be retained for a limited period for security and integrity, then deleted or anonymized.
- **Legal retention:** we may retain data longer where required by law.
